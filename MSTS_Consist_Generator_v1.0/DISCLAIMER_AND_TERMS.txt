1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------
MSTS Consist Generator is a community-made utility intended to help users create Microsoft Train Simulator (.con) consist files from locomotive (.eng) and wagon/vehicle (.wag) files available in a user's selected Trainset folder. It is provided as a convenience tool. It does not replace the user's responsibility to maintain, back up, configure, and test their MSTS installation.
